Empire of
the Limit

A philosophical science fiction cycle spanning nine novels.

In a civilization where memory, value, and order are sustained by a single system, people trust it as naturally as air or light. But what happens when that system begins to return contradictory answers?

Empire of the Limit follows a civilization trying to preserve a shared order while gradually discovering that no system can fully replace the living connection between one person and another.

The Cycle

Three trilogies. Nine novels. One world.

Empire of the Limit explores memory, power, human worth, and the recurring attempt to build a universal order that can outlive its creators.

I

Crisis of the Limit

The system that has preserved humanity’s memory and maintained the Empire’s order for centuries begins to return contradictory answers. The first trilogy follows the collapse of a universal order, the price of preserving it, and the search for a new form of connection.

II

Crisis of Measure

Centuries later, society attempts to build a system in which every person’s place is determined solely by ability, decisions, and contribution. But can a human being be measured without being distorted?

III

Crisis of the Human Limit

The final trilogy turns to humanity’s need for an order capable of outliving individual lives. Can people exist without a single truth, a common structure, or the promise of guaranteed connection?

Trilogy I · Crisis of the Limit

Order was eternal. Until it stopped answering.

When the system that defines the value of every action begins to behave inconsistently, the people of the Empire confront the impossible. Some fight to restore the old balance. Others begin to ask whether the Limit has exhausted the very principle on which it was built.

Q4 2026

Reconstruction of the Limit

After familiar answers disappear, the Empire tries to recover its lost balance. Yet every attempt to save the system changes the world it was meant to preserve.

2027

New Limit

The old order cannot be restored. The question is no longer how to return to it, but whether a new foundation for unity can be built without repeating its failures.

Series Roadmap

The story knows its direction. It does not promise easy answers.

The first trilogy covers the next releases. The following two move the world centuries forward and shift the conflict from the failure of a system to the failure of the very idea of universal order.

About the Author

Petr Samarov

Petr Samarov is a writer of philosophical science fiction based in Germany.

For more than a decade, he worked with learning, talent development, and organizational systems in international technology companies. That experience became part of the foundation of Empire of the Limit.

His fiction explores memory, power, human worth, freedom of choice, and the limits of our belief in universal order.
