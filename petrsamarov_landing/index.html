Petr Samarov · Science Fiction

Empire of the Limit

Philosophical science fiction in nine novels.

A civilization has learned to trust the system that preserves memory, assigns value, and keeps order as naturally as it trusts air or light. Then the system begins to contradict itself.

Empire of the Limit follows people trying to preserve a common order while discovering that no system can fully replace the living, imperfect connection between one person and another.

The Cycle

Three trilogies.
Nine novels.
One world.

The cycle moves from the collapse of a universal system, to the impossibility of measuring a person without distortion, and finally to the human need to build an order that can outlive us.

I

Crisis of the Limit

The system that has preserved memory and sustained the Empire for centuries begins to return contradictory answers. The first trilogy follows the collapse of universal order, the price of preserving it, and the search for another way to connect human beings.

II

Crisis of Measure

A future society attempts to build a system of absolute meritocracy and objective human evaluation. The more precise the system becomes, the more clearly its own limits appear.

III

Crisis of the Human Limit

The final trilogy turns to the question beneath every earlier crisis: why do people build systems designed to survive them, and what remains when no universal order is guaranteed?

Trilogy I · Crisis of the Limit

The first trilogy begins when the Limit — the structure that has defined the value of action for generations — starts behaving inconsistently. Some try to restore it. Others begin to ask whether the order they trusted ever existed in the form they imagined.

Author

Petr Samarov

Petr Samarov writes philosophical science fiction about memory, power, value, freedom, and the systems people create to make the world legible.

Before turning to fiction, he spent more than a decade working with learning, people development, and organizational systems in international technology companies. He lives in Germany.
